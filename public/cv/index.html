Sunken History of Grand Lake

2017

Lake Maps NL set out to map Grand Lake’s shoreline from before a 1920s dam raised the water 11 metres. The sonar picked up what looked like the sunken railway bridge, but with too many piers. Archive photos revealed a forgotten temporary dam just upstream, and side-scan sonar later captured both, including all 21 of the dam’s piers. I co-wrote this paper about the find with 4 other members, published in The Journal of Ocean Technology (volume 12, number 4).
