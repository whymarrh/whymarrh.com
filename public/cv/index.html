Sunken History of Grand Lake

2017

Lake Maps NL set out to map Grand Lake’s shoreline from before a 1920s dam raised the water eleven metres. Sonar of the sunken railway bridge showed too many piers, and archive photos revealed a forgotten temporary dam about fifty metres upstream. I co-wrote this paper about our finding with four other members, published in The Journal of Ocean Technology (volume 12, number 4).
